Presentation

I am an ISFP researcher at Inria Sophia Antipolis, in the MARIANNE team at I3S (Université Côte d’Azur / CNRS / Inria), since October 2023.

After completing my PhD at IRIT in Toulouse (2021) on similarity in argumentation, I held postdoctoral positions at CNAM Paris (2021–2022), working on uncertain and temporal knowledge graphs, and at the University of Perugia (2022–2023), working on temporal and probabilistic argumentation.

My research focuses on computational argumentation, knowledge representation, logic and neuro-symbolic AI, particularly implicit arguments, similarity, persuasion, argument quality and explainable reasoning.

I coordinate the Inria international associated team EXPLAINER, devoted to understanding and reconstructing implicit arguments.
