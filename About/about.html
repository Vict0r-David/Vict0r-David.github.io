About

Current position

Since October 2023 — ISFP Researcher (Inria Starting Faculty Position), Inria Sophia Antipolis, MARIANNE team, I3S (UMR 7271), Université Côte d’Azur / CNRS / Inria.

My research focuses on computational argumentation, knowledge representation and reasoning, neuro-symbolic AI, similarity, persuasion and implicit reasoning.

Research experience

  • 2022–2023: Postdoctoral researcher, University of Perugia (KRARLab), temporal, probabilistic and explainable argumentation.
  • 2021–2022: Postdoctoral researcher, CNAM Paris (CEDRIC), MAP inference in uncertain and temporal knowledge graphs.
  • 2017–2021: PhD, IRIT, Université Toulouse III – Paul Sabatier, supervised by Leila Amgoud — Dealing with Similarity in Argumentation.
  • 2017: M2 research internship at IRIT on ranking-based argumentation semantics.

Education

  • 2017–2021 — PhD in Computer Science, Université Toulouse III – Paul Sabatier.
  • 2015–2017 — MSc in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Pattern Recognition.
  • 2012–2015 — BSc in Computer Science, Université Toulouse III – Paul Sabatier.
