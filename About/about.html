Current position

ISFP Researcher (Inria Starting Faculty Position), October 2023–present
Inria Sophia Antipolis, I3S (UMR 7271), MARIANNE team — Université Côte d’Azur / CNRS / Inria.

Research in computational argumentation, knowledge representation and reasoning, neuro-symbolic AI, similarity, persuasion and implicit reasoning. My work develops symbolic and computational methods for understanding arguments, reasoning with uncertainty and incompleteness, and analysing persuasion, disinformation and manipulation in debate.

Research experience

  • 2022–2023 — Postdoctoral Researcher, KRARLab, University of Perugia: temporal, probabilistic and explainable argumentation.
  • 2021–2022 — Postdoctoral Researcher, CEDRIC, CNAM Paris: uncertain and temporal knowledge graphs, MAP inference.
  • 2017–2021 — PhD, IRIT, Université Toulouse III – Paul Sabatier, supervised by Leila Amgoud: Dealing with Similarity in Argumentation.
  • 2017 — M2 internship, IRIT: ranking-based argumentation semantics.
