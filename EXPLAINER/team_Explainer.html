EXPLAINER (Inria–UCL Team)

EXPLAINER is an Inria international associated team established with Anthony Hunter (University College London). It studies implicit arguments and neuro-symbolic reasoning.

Following the tragic loss of Anthony Hunter, Antonio Rago (King’s College London) has taken over as UK co-PI. Victor David coordinates the Inria side.

In memory of Tony Hunter

Tony was not only an exceptional researcher, but also a dear friend and a mentor to me. His kindness, generosity and intellectual curiosity made him a truly remarkable person. He inspired so many people throughout the argumentation community, and his influence will continue to live on through the people he supported and the ideas he shared. I will always be grateful for everything I learned from him and for the friendship we shared.

Scientific objectives

Human communication frequently leaves premises or conclusions unstated. These implicit components, or enthymemes, raise challenges for understanding and evaluating arguments. EXPLAINER develops logical representations, argument graphs and neuro-symbolic methods to reconstruct and assess them.

  • Identify and represent implicit components of natural-language arguments.
  • Decode and compare multiple plausible interpretations.
  • Evaluate reconstruction quality and its consequences for argumentative relations.
  • Develop interpretable approaches combining logic and language models.
